# Meridian — Sales Rep Training Deck

`Meridian-Sales-Training.pptx` — a 16-slide, presentation-ready deck for training sales
reps on what Meridian is and how to sell it to business owners.

Built from the internal **Meridian Bible** (the one-liner, pricing, scripts, and objection
responses), it's aimed at reps who pitch owners on the floor. Every slide includes speaker
notes.

## Agenda

| # | Slide | Purpose |
|---|-------|---------|
| 1 | Title | Framing: what reps will walk out able to do |
| 2 | What Is Meridian? | The one-liner + "no hardware" |
| 3 | Three Kinds of Intelligence | POS · Camera · Fusion |
| 4 | Why Owners Can't Say No | The core value to a business owner |
| 5 | Flying Blind → Seeing Everything | Sell the transformation |
| 6 | It's *Not* Just a Phone Agent | Kill the #1 misconception |
| 7 | What Meridian Actually Shows Them | The dashboard metrics |
| 8 | Fusion Intelligence | Our moat + the 23%-traffic example |
| 9 | Who Benefits Most | Target verticals + best-fit signals |
| 10 | It Pays for Itself 10x Over | The money math |
| 11 | Three Simple Plans | Pricing quick reference |
| 12 | The Questions Every Owner Asks | General Q&A |
| 13–14 | Handling Objections (Parts 1–2) | Word-for-word responses |
| 15 | Read-Only, Private, Reversible | Disarm the trust objection |
| 16 | Go Close Deals | Recap + call to action |

## Covers the four training goals

- **Why Meridian is great for business owners** — slides 4, 5, 10
- **Why it's not just a phone agent** — slide 6 (+ recap)
- **Which businesses benefit most** — slide 9
- **Major questions & objections from owners** — slides 12, 13, 14, 15

## Regenerating

The deck is generated from `scripts/`-style source kept in the session scratchpad
(`deck.js` + `render-icons.js`, using `pptxgenjs`). Ask to regenerate if the source of
truth (pricing, plans, scripts) changes.
